What gets copied, and what does not
The facts
Car, price, suburb, who pays what. Copied — facts are not anybody's property, and they are what the filters run on.
The wording
Not copied. Write your own line. The paragraph they wrote belongs to them; the link is how we credit it.
The photos
Not copied. Same reason.
The phone number
Never. They put it in a group they chose, not on a site they have never heard of. Numbers are stripped out of the summary automatically, and the listing carries the link instead.
