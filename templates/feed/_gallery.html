{% comment %} The photos on a post: one, or up to six you can step through. A SCROLLER FIRST, A CAROUSEL SECOND The track is an ordinary horizontal scroll area with CSS scroll snapping, and the arrows only call scrollBy on it. That order matters on the phones this is read on: swiping works because the browser is doing the scrolling, not because a script is listening for touches. With JavaScript broken or still loading you can still swipe through every photo — the arrows are the part that degrades, and they hide themselves when there is nothing to step to. ONE PHOTO IS NOT A CAROUSEL No arrows, no counter, no dots when there is one image, which is the common case. Controls that cannot do anything are worse than no controls, because somebody has to press one to find that out.
