What happens to your document

Somebody reads it, then it is deleted. Approving or rejecting destroys the file in the same action — there is no button in our admin that keeps it.

What we keep is a date and a tick. That your ID was checked, and when. Never the document, never the number on it.

If you never finish, it still goes. Anything left unreviewed is deleted automatically after 30 days.
