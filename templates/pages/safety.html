Staying safe

Most people on here are straight with you. The ones who are not all work the same few ways, and they are easy to spot once you know the shape of it.

Never pay anything before you have seen the car in person. No genuine owner needs money to hold a vehicle for you. Not a deposit, not a "booking fee", not airtime, not a courier charge for keys. If money is asked for before you have stood next to the car, it is a scam. Every time.
If you are looking for a car

Meet in daylight, somewhere public. A filling station forecourt or a shopping centre car park. Not a house, not after dark, and not somewhere you have never been.

Check the licence disc against their ID. The name on the disc should be the person in front of you, or they should be able to explain who owns it and why they are letting it. "It is my cousin's" is not an explanation, it is a warning.

Read the agreement before you sign it. Especially who pays for what when something breaks, and what happens if the car is impounded or crashed. If there is no written agreement, there is no agreement.

Be careful about pressure. "Three other drivers want it, pay now to hold it" is a technique, not a fact.

If you are letting a car

See the licence and the PrDP, and check the dates. An expired PrDP means your insurance may not pay out, and it is you who carries that.

Phone the previous owner they drove for. Not a reference they wrote you — the actual person, on a number you found yourself. Ten minutes on the phone tells you more than any document.

Do not hand over the car and the papers on the same day you meet. Take your time. Anybody genuine will understand why.

Keep the tracker on and in your name. A driver who objects to a tracker is telling you something.

What the badges mean, exactly

Phone verified means we sent a code to that number and somebody typed it back. It proves the number reaches them. It proves nothing else.

ID verified means a person here looked at an identity document and it matched the name on the account. We do not keep it.

Licence verified means the same for a driver's licence, and that it had not expired when we looked.

None of it is a character reference. A verified account is a real person we can identify, which matters a great deal if something goes wrong. It is not a promise that they will be good to deal with. Do your own checking as well.

If something goes wrong

Report it. There is a report link on every listing and every profile. We do not tell the person who reported them.

Block them if you simply want to be left alone. They are not told, they disappear from your search results, and neither of you can ask the other for an introduction again.

If money has changed hands, go to the police. Open a case and get the case number. We can suspend an account and we will, but we cannot get your money back and we will not pretend otherwise.

What we do and do not do

We do not hold your money. No payments run through this site, so nobody here can refund you. Anyone claiming to offer TrustHailer escrow is not us.

We never ask for your sign-in code. Not by phone, not on WhatsApp, not ever. Anyone who does is trying to take your account.

We do not publish accusations. When somebody is reported we investigate and suspend accounts where it is warranted. We do not run a public list of names — those get weaponised, and the wrong name on one ruins somebody who did nothing.
